# Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built in Power BI Desktop to analyze mobile retail sales performance across Indian cities, smartphone brands, payment methods, and time periods.

## Dashboard Preview

![Dashboard Overview](Dashboard%20Overview.png)

## Key Metrics & Visuals

- **Executive KPIs:** High-level summary cards displaying Total Sales, Total Quantity, Transactions, and Customer Ratings.
- **Geographic Sales Distribution:** Interactive map visualizing Total Sales by City across India.
- **Brand & Model Breakdown:** Comparative analysis of sales, quantity, and transactions across brands (Motorola, Xiaomi, Vivo, OnePlus, Samsung, and Apple) and individual mobile models.
- **Time-Series Trends:** Trend visuals tracking Total Quantity by Month/Day and Total Sales by Day Name.
- **Customer & Payment Insights:** Customer Ratings distribution funnel alongside Transactions by Payment Method (UPI, Cash, Debit Card, and Credit Card).
- **Dynamic Slicers:** Interactive filtering by Month, Mobile Model, Payment Method, Brand, and Day Name.

## Tools & Skills Used

- **Power BI Desktop:** Data modeling, interactive report design, and cross-filtering
- **Data Visualization:** KPI cards, maps, line charts, bar charts, funnel charts, and donut charts

## How to Use

1. Download `Mobile sales dashboard.pbix` from this repository.
2. Open the file in **Power BI Desktop** to interact with the slicers, tooltips, and visuals.
